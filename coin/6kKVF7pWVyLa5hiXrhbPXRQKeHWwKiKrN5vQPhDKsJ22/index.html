What this is not

The fund is this coin's own fee revenue and nothing else. It is first come, first served, it can run dry, and a coin with no trading has nothing to pay with. Two wallets trading against each other can manufacture a fall, which is why cover is capped at a third of it, why the mark price is slow, and why the creator is never covered. This is not insurance and not a promise about returns.
