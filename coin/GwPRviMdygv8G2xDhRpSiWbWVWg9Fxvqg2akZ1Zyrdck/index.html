What happens next

Everything that has reached the vault is already booked. The next settle happens once more fees arrive, and anyone may press it.

209 epochs published so far. Each one is a merkle root over every holder's time-weighted balance, claimable the moment it lands.

SettleBooks what has arrived: 0% to the creator, 20% to the Basket treasury, 1% to you for pressing it, the rest to holders.

Trades go straight to ponsfamily.com's program. Every transaction is simulated before your wallet opens; the price you see is the curve's own maths from its live reserves. This coin is quoted in PUMP, so both sides of a trade are PUMP.

Basket

A launchpad on ponsfamily.com. A coin's creator fees are frozen to a program-owned vault at launch and paid to its holders in real assets: one asset the coin is quoted in, or a basket of up to eight. The house coin is $BASKET.
