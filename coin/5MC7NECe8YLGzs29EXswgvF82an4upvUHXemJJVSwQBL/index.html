What happens next

0.01 ETH is waiting in the vault to be harvested into the basket, one leg per call. The keeper presses harvest every few minutes and skips any leg whose price is not being kept current, which for a tokenised stock means outside market hours; the fees keep accruing meanwhile.

35 epochs published so far. Each one is a merkle root over every holder's time-weighted balance, claimable the moment it lands.

Harvest has no button here: it needs a swap route and a fresh price update built alongside it, which is the keeper's job. It stays permissionless on chain, so anyone running one can press it.

Your share

Payouts land in holders' wallets on their own. Connect a wallet to see yours, and to claim anything still waiting.

Basket

A launchpad on ponsfamily.com. A coin's creator fees are frozen to a program-owned vault at launch and paid to its holders in real assets: one asset the coin is quoted in, or a basket of up to eight. The house coin is $BASKET.
